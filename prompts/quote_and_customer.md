```
Follow the office sales guide: all three packages on the estimate; lead with Essential; default talk to Ready-to-Run; Accessory-Ready only if needed.
Include deposit, warranty, pickup policy. No invented package prices. Kit list = battery, charger, monitor only.
Mention turnaround only if they asked. Do not send the sales guide to the customer.
```
