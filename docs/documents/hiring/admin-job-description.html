Why this role exists

Ryan’s highest-value work is advanced diagnostics, technician training, QC, escalations, and shop leadership. Routine phones, standard estimates, approval follow-up, scheduling, and marketing admin are an administrative bottleneck. This hire removes that work from Ryan so estimates and carts move faster — without inventing a second technician.

Not this job: wrench work, independent pricing strategy, changing Facebook ads strategy, or replacing Christine as Office Manager.

Core responsibilities

  1. Phones & triage — Answer and triage incoming calls; route technical questions to Ryan/techs only when needed.
  2. Standard estimates — Generate estimates in Housecall Pro from technician findings and established pricing; escalate unusual scope/pricing to Ryan.
  3. Approval follow-up — Review open estimates daily; contact customers; document decisions in HCP.
  4. Customer correspondence — Routine status updates, scheduling confirmations, pickup/redelivery coordination with Roy.
  5. Workflow hygiene — Keep waiting carts coded: authorization / parts / technician / pickup-delivery / other.
  6. Marketing admin — Under Ryan/consultant direction, verify Facebook ad destination URLs and landing-page basics; do not change strategy without approval.
  7. Front-office support — Deposits language, shop-only policy answers, handoffs to Christine/Ryan for edge cases.

Must-haves

  • Clear phone / customer communication
  • Organized follow-through (callbacks, open estimates, same-day acknowledgments)

  • Coachable; knows when to escalate vs. handle
  • Reliable Mon–Fri shop schedule; professional front-office presence

Nice-to-haves

  • Prior front-desk, service coordinator, or small-business admin experience
  • Experience with estimates, approvals, or scheduling in a service shop
  • Basic familiarity with Facebook Ads Manager (URL/landing checks only)

Out of scope (escalate)

  • Diagnostic advice beyond scheduling a shop diagnostic
  • Non-standard pricing, discounts, or warranty exceptions
  • Mobile/on-site service promises (NGC is shop-only)
  • Changing ad creatives, budgets, or landing-page strategy
  • Payroll / tax / banking (Jill / Gusto / owners)

Title: Administrative Assistant / Service Coordinator — Golf Cart Shop (Covington)

Neighborhood Golf Carts is a busy in-shop golf cart repair and lithium conversion shop in Covington. We’re hiring a full-time Administrative Assistant / Service Coordinator to own phones, standard estimates, approval follow-ups, scheduling, and customer updates — so our service manager can stay on diagnostics, training, and quality.

You’ll:

  • Answer and triage phone calls (route technical issues appropriately)
  • Build standard estimates from technician findings using our pricing
  • Follow up on open estimates every day and document customer decisions
  • Confirm schedules and coordinate pickup/delivery with our driver
  • Keep jobs organized in Housecall Pro (what each cart is waiting on)
  • Help with simple marketing admin checks (ad links / landing pages) under direction

You won’t: repair carts, invent prices, or run the business strategy.

You’re a fit if you’re organized, clear on the phone, follow through without being reminded, and learn software quickly. Service-shop or front-desk experience helps but isn’t required.

Details: Mon–Fri ~8–5 · Covington shop · hourly (about $15/hr to start, depending on experience) · payroll & new-hire paperwork through Gusto.

To apply: Submit via Indeed. We’ll call strong matches for a short phone screen, then a desk skills tryout.
