# Lithium iron phosphate (LFP) pack recovery

**Last verified:** 2026-09-18  
**Owner:** Diagnostics (reports to Chief). Shop technicians run the work on the cart.  
**Source:** Ryan confirmed 2026-09-18 via Chief.

Shop procedure for Neighborhood Golf Carts. Do **not** invent other cell voltages or warranty claims.

## Standing rules

1. Lithium iron phosphate (LFP) packs cannot be “recovered” when the cells have gone below **1.50 volts** and the battery management system is offline.
2. The Vatrer “suitcase” charger is only meant to recover packs that are just below the **2.500 volt** cutoff **with a live battery management system**. It raises charge enough that the pack’s original equipment charger can pick up and continue to a full charge.
3. Cells between **1.00 and 1.50 volts** require close attention: record the recovery attempt, then test to see if the cells were damaged.
4. Cells below **1.00 volt** are not recoverable in the field.
