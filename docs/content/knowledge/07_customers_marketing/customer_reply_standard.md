# Shop Customer-Reply Standard (no PII)

**Last verified:** 2026-08-23  
**Owner:** Ryan White  
**Audience:** Front Desk (Jesse), Chief, shop bots

Use this when a customer asks **what is in the lithium kit**, **what the convenience package includes**, or about **battery quality / warranty**. Facts: [lithium_conversions.md](../02_products/lithium_conversions.md).

**No customer PII** in drafts stored here or in chat logs you keep in this brain — names, phones, addresses, and invoice numbers stay in Housecall Pro.

## Hard rules

- **Kit list is short:** LiFePO4 battery, charger, and monitor. Do not recite the longer HCP / QBO “Included Equipment” list.
- **Vatrer:** We use Vatrer packs. We inspect, test, and tune every lithium we install.
- **Warranty:** Battery and BMS — **5-year full replacement**.
- **Convenience package:** Quote HCP item `3.0-NGC Lithium Conversion Convenience Package` at **$599 plus tax** only. Do not unbundle it into the old à-la-carte charger-port / reducer / fuse-box talk track unless the live estimate already has those separate lines.
- **EcoBoost:** Do not mention EcoBoost unless that **exact line** is on the live estimate.
- **No shop phone** on these replies. Close with **Thanks / Neighborhood Golf Carts team**.
- **Turnaround:** Do not invent or volunteer a timeline unless the customer asked.
- After approval on deposit jobs: queue pickup or drop-off. Do **not** say lock a time, hold a spot, or easy yes. Deposit before ordering battery / motor / controller.

## SMS / template — kit, quality, warranty

```
The lithium kit is a LiFePO4 battery, charger, and monitor. We use Vatrer packs and inspect, test, and tune every lithium we install. Battery and BMS are covered by a 5-year full replacement warranty.

Thanks,
Neighborhood Golf Carts team
```

## SMS / template — convenience package

```
The convenience package is $599 plus tax. It includes an AC charge port with a 15' extension cord, a 20A key-switched DC/DC converter (48V to 12V, shuts off with the key), and a 6-position 12V accessory fuse box.

Thanks,
Neighborhood Golf Carts team
```

## Combined (kit + convenience, if they asked both)

```
The lithium kit is a LiFePO4 battery, charger, and monitor. We use Vatrer packs and inspect, test, and tune every lithium we install. Battery and BMS: 5-year full replacement warranty.

The convenience package is $599 plus tax: AC charge port + 15' extension cord; 20A key-switched DC/DC (48V to 12V, shuts off with the key); and a 6-position 12V accessory fuse box.

Thanks,
Neighborhood Golf Carts team
```

Use the live estimate for the kit voltage/SKU and whether the convenience package is already on the quote. Do not add EcoBoost, a phone number, or a promised date unless they asked and you have a real shop answer.
