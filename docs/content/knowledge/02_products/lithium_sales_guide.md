## Goal

Win the job against cheaper quotes **and** raise the ticket without selling parts the cart does not need.

- **Lead with Essential** so we look competitive.
- **Default the customer to Ready-to-Run.** That is the number you talk about most.
- **Add Accessory-Ready only** when it solves a real problem.

## The three packages

| Package | Role | What is in it | When to sell |
|---------|------|---------------|--------------|
| **Essential** | Comparison price / battery swap only | Lithium battery, onboard charger, state-of-charge monitor, installation labor | Cart already has a compatible converter, **or** has no 12V accessories at all |
| **Ready-to-Run** | **DEFAULT QUOTE** / the finished conversion | Everything in Essential + key-switch DC-DC converter + charge port + extension cord | Most street, neighborhood, and lifted carts. **This is what most people should buy.** |
| **Accessory-Ready** | **UPSELL** / future-proof wiring | Everything in Ready-to-Run + fuse box on the converter + clean tap points for add-ons | Cart already has lights / stereo / USB, **or** the customer plans to add accessories |

Put **all three packages on the estimate** so the higher ticket is a choice. Do not advertise only the loaded price. Do not hide extras until after they approve a low number.

## What each part does

| Part | Need vs want | Default | Notes |
|------|----------------|---------|-------|
| **Battery + charger + monitor** | The conversion itself | **Always required. Always default.** | Customer-facing kit = LiFePO4 battery, charger, and battery monitor. Vatrer packs; inspect, test, and tune on site. |
| **Key-switch DC-DC converter** | Need on most carts | **Yes**, unless the cart already has a good compatible one | Steps 36V/48V down to 12V so lights, horn, radio, dash still work. Key-switch so it does not drain when the cart is off. |
| **Charge port + extension** | Want that feels like need | **Yes** on street / neighborhood carts | Factory-location port. Onboard charger stays in the battery box. Customer plugs in like a normal cart. |
| **Fuse box** | Want / future-proof | **Only if** accessories exist or are planned | Clean 12V taps off the converter so the customer adds accessories later without splicing. |

Skip the converter if they already have a compatible one — that builds trust.

## Inspect before you quote

- Does the cart have lights, horn, radio, USB, or other 12V accessories?
- How is 12V currently fed: factory converter, key-switch converter, or tapped off individual batteries?
- If there is a converter, will it accept the lithium pack voltage range?
- Is the factory charge port still in place, and will the customer want to plug in like a normal cart?
- Are accessory wires already spliced / messy? That is a fuse-box conversation.
- Street / neighborhood use vs bare course cart? Charge-port value is higher on street carts.

Do not lock a package until that inspect is done.

## Open with

> We can do the battery swap only, or we can leave the cart the way a finished conversion should be: 12V accessories still work, charging is plug-and-play, and there is a clean place to add anything later. Most people do Ready-to-Run.

## Say this, not that

### Converter

- **SAY:** Your lights and radio used to pull 12 volts off one battery. Lithium is one pack. Without a converter those circuits do not have a safe 12V source. We use a key-switch unit so it is not drawing when the cart is off.
- **AVOID:** You should add a converter / a lot of people get one.

### Charge port

- **SAY:** The charger stays on the cart. We put a new port in the factory location. You plug in an extension like a normal golf cart instead of fishing a cord out of the battery box.
- **AVOID:** We also sell charge ports if you want one.

### Fuse box

- **SAY:** Anything you add later lands on this block. No splicing into the converter leads.
- **AVOID:** The full package includes a fuse box.

### Price vs competitors

- **SAY:** Their quote is the battery and charger. Ours can be that too — that is Essential. Ready-to-Run is the same conversion plus the parts that keep lights working and charging simple. Compare those two lists, not just the first number.
- **AVOID:** We include more so we cost more.

## Office rules

### Do

- Inspect before locking a package.
- Put all three packages on the estimate so the higher ticket is a choice.
- Default checkboxes: Essential + converter (if needed) + charge port.
- Skip the converter if they already have a compatible one (builds trust).
- Use Ready-to-Run as the number you talk about most.
- If they only approve Essential, still warn them about 12V accessories **in writing**.

### Don't

- Advertise only the loaded price.
- Hide extras until after they approve a low number.
- Force a fuse box on a cart with no accessories and no plans.
- Sell Essential on a cart that taps 12V off individual batteries and then act surprised when lights die.
- Invent a need.
- Argue price — argue what is in the box and what still works on day one.

## How this relates to the old convenience package

HCP still has `3.0-NGC Lithium Conversion Convenience Package` at **$599 plus tax**. That SKU bundles **converter + charge port + fuse box** (all three extras). That mix is closest to **Accessory-Ready extras**, **not** Ready-to-Run.

- **Do not** auto-add the $599 bundle as the default quote.
- **Do not** call the $599 SKU Ready-to-Run (Ready-to-Run has no fuse box).
- **Do not** invent new Essential / Ready-to-Run / Accessory-Ready prices.
- Build the estimate from the Professional Kit SKU plus the live HCP lines that match what the cart actually needs (converter, charge port, fuse box — only if needed).
- Use the $599 convenience-package SKU only when the live estimate already has that line, or when Accessory-Ready is the honest recommendation **and** that SKU is how Jesse is ringing the three extras today.

## Customer-facing facts that stay true

These are still true. They are **not** a reason to send this sales guide to the customer.

- Kit = LiFePO4 battery, charger, and battery monitor. We use **Vatrer** packs. We **inspect, test, and tune** every lithium we install.
- Warranty: **5-year full replacement** battery + BMS.
- Typical install: **2–3 business days** (sometimes same day). Do not invent or volunteer a timeline in customer replies unless they asked.
- After conversion, the **factory battery meter is no longer accurate**. The kit includes a **battery monitor** for state of charge.
- The **speedometer still works**.

Do not mention EcoBoost unless that exact line is on the live estimate.
