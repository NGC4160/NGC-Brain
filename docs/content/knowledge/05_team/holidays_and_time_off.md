# Holidays and time off

**Last verified:** 2026-10-03  
**Owner:** Betty (HR) via Chief  
**Confirmed by:** Ryan White

Neighborhood Golf Carts paid time off is **only** the paid holidays on this page. There is **no PTO**.

Betty owns this file. Do **not** invent vacation days, sick-pay banks, or other paid-leave programs.

## Paid holidays (3 per person)

**Confirmed 2026-09-29 by Ryan White.**

Thanksgiving and Christmas Day are paid shop-closed days, plus one floating paid day each person picks from Day After Thanksgiving, Christmas Eve, or New Year’s Day (**3 paid total**).

| Kind | Days | Pay | Shop |
|------|------|-----|------|
| Always paid | **Thanksgiving**, **Christmas Day** | Paid | Closed. Nobody works. |
| Floating (pick one) | **Day After Thanksgiving**, **Christmas Eve**, or **New Year’s Day** | Paid | Closed that day **if everyone picks the same floating day**. |

- Each person picks **exactly one** floating day.
- **Nobody works a paid holiday.**
- Total = **3 paid holidays** per person.

This supersedes older handbook / compensation wording that said unpaid holidays only, or that paid holidays may be added later, for these three paid days.

## Unpaid closed days

**Memorial Day**, **Independence Day**, **Labor Day**, and **Veterans Day** remain **unpaid** closed days.

## Eligibility

To be paid for a paid holiday, the person must work the **full scheduled shift before and after** the holiday.

Excused only for:

- sick leave with notice, or
- other time off Ryan approved

## No PTO

**Confirmed 2026-10-03 by Ryan White.**

Neighborhood Golf Carts has **no PTO**. Paid time off is only the paid holidays.

Holiday-pay wording must **not** say “approved PTO”.
