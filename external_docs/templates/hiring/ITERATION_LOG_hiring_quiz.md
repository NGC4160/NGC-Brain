# Hiring Quiz — Loop Engineering Log

**Target:** 40 questions · 120 pts (3 ea) · ~45–60 min · pass **75% (90/120)**  
**Bar:** Entry-level / Day-1 shop helper · transferable skill · no golf-cart brand trivia  
**Pairs with:** Phone scorecard → Hands-on eval scorecard

---

## Loop 1 — Scaffold domains

**Critique:** Need full coverage matching phone/hands-on themes.  
**Change:** Outline 40 slots across Safety (8), Meter/electrical (8), Batteries (8), Process/troubleshoot (8), Mechanical/shop (5), Lithium awareness light (3).  
**Result:** Balanced blueprint; lithium kept light (hire-teachable).

## Loop 2 — Kill jargon & brand trivia

**Critique:** Words like HPD, SepEx, Curtis, specific gravity deep-dives fail trainable auto/marine techs unfairly.  
**Change:** Rewrite in plain Ask language; replace OEM terms with “direction switch,” “main contactor/solenoid,” “battery water acid type vs lithium.”  
**Result:** Readable by someone who never touched a cart.

## Loop 3 — Discriminate parts-swappers

**Critique:** Too many recall facts; won’t catch “replace the controller first.”  
**Change:** Add ~8 “what do you check next?” / “what does this reading mean?” items. Wrong answers = shotgun parts.  
**Result:** Process weight matches phone screen Sections B–D.

## Loop 4 — Safety must-get-right set

**Critique:** Someone can pass on luck while being unsafe.  
**Change:** Flag 6 **critical safety** items (Q1–Q6). Scorecard note: miss **2+ critical** → written fail regardless of total (mirrors live Safety gate).  
**Result:** Aligns with phone E + hands-on S gate.

## Loop 5 — Distractor quality

**Critique:** Some wrong answers were silly (“paint color”).  
**Change:** Make every wrong choice a mistake a real tech might make (trust key-off, continuity = cable is fine under load, lithium = lead-acid charger OK if volts match).  
**Result:** Wrong answers diagnose *how* they think.

## Loop 6 — Difficulty curve & timing

**Critique:** Hard items clustered at front; fatigue risk.  
**Change:** Easy→medium wave: Safety openers, then meter, batteries, process scenarios, light lithium, wrap with shop sense. Target ~75 sec/question average.  
**Result:** Finishes in 45–60 min for solid candidates.

## Loop 7 — Align to hands-on stations

**Critique:** Written shouldn’t duplicate what H1–H4 already prove, but should preview them.  
**Change:** Written asks *knowledge* of meter settings, connection problems, walk-around items; leave *demonstration* to bay. Remove “perform LOTO steps in order” essay — that’s live.  
**Result:** No double-jeopardy; written + hands-on complement.

## Loop 8 — Final polish & answer key audit

**Critique:** Ambiguous stems, double keys, uneven point value.  
**Change:** One best answer each; all 3 pts; answer key + short rationale; pass/fail rules on cover; version 1.0 locked.  
**Result:** Ready for shop use. Stop loops — criteria met.
