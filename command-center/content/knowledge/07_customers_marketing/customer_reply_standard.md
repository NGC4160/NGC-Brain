**Last verified:** 2026-08-30

**Audience:** Front Desk (Jesse), Chief, shop bots

Use this when a customer asks **what is in the lithium kit**, **battery quality / warranty**, or **what still works after conversion**. Facts: [lithium_conversions.md](../02_products/lithium_conversions.md).

**Office quoting** (Essential / Ready-to-Run / Accessory-Ready): [lithium_sales_guide.md](../02_products/lithium_sales_guide.md). That guide is **internal only** — do not send it to the customer. Inspect first. Recommend only what that cart needs.

**No customer PII** in drafts stored here or in chat logs you keep in this brain — names, phones, addresses, and invoice numbers stay in Housecall Pro.

## Hard rules

- **Kit list is short:** LiFePO4 battery, charger, and monitor. Do not recite the longer HCP / QBO “Included Equipment” list.
- **Vatrer:** We use Vatrer packs. We inspect, test, and tune every lithium we install.
- **Warranty:** Battery and BMS — **5-year full replacement**.
- **After conversion:** Factory battery meter is no longer accurate (kit includes a battery monitor). Speedometer still works.
- **Quoting extras:** Follow [lithium_sales_guide.md](../02_products/lithium_sales_guide.md). Lead with Essential, default the customer to Ready-to-Run, add Accessory-Ready only when accessories exist or are planned. Do **not** default-pitch the $599 convenience package as the finished conversion. Do not invent package prices.
- **If they ask about the convenience package SKU:** HCP `3.0-NGC Lithium Conversion Convenience Package` is still **$599 plus tax** (charge port + 15' cord; 20A key-switched DC/DC; 6-position fuse box). That line is all three extras — closest to Accessory-Ready extras, not Ready-to-Run. Confirm only if it is on the live estimate or they asked about that SKU by name.
- **EcoBoost:** Do not mention EcoBoost unless that **exact line** is on the live estimate.
- **No shop phone** on these replies. Close with **Thanks / Neighborhood Golf Carts team**.
- **Turnaround:** Typical is 2–3 business days. Do not invent or volunteer a timeline unless the customer asked.
- After approval on deposit jobs: queue pickup or drop-off. Do **not** say lock a time, hold a spot, or easy yes. Deposit before ordering battery / motor / controller.

## SMS / template — kit, quality, warranty

```
The lithium kit is a LiFePO4 battery, charger, and monitor. We use Vatrer packs and inspect, test, and tune every lithium we install. Battery and BMS are covered by a 5-year full replacement warranty. After conversion the factory battery meter is no longer accurate — the kit monitor shows state of charge. The speedometer still works.

Thanks,
Neighborhood Golf Carts team
```

## SMS / template — they asked about extras / “what else do I need?”

Do not send the sales guide. Do not dump all three packages with invented prices. Use the live estimate after inspect. Short customer text:

```
The kit is the battery, charger, and monitor. Most street and neighborhood carts also need a key-switch 12V converter so lights and radio still have a safe 12V source, and a factory-location charge port so you plug in like a normal cart. We only recommend a fuse block if you already have accessories or plan to add them. We’ll put the options on the estimate after we see the cart.

Thanks,
Neighborhood Golf Carts team
```

## SMS / template — they asked about the $599 convenience package SKU by name

```
That line is $599 plus tax. It is an AC charge port with a 15' extension cord, a 20A key-switched DC/DC converter (48V to 12V, shuts off with the key), and a 6-position 12V accessory fuse box. We only add the fuse box when the cart already has accessories or you plan to add them. Use the estimate for what this cart actually needs.

Thanks,
Neighborhood Golf Carts team
```

Use the live estimate for the kit voltage/SKU and which extras are on the quote. Do not add EcoBoost, a phone number, or a promised date unless they asked and you have a real shop answer.
