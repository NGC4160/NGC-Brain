# Parts board

**Last verified:** 2026-10-09  
**Owner:** Parts via Chief  
**Confirmed by:** Ryan White (2026-10-02 attribution; 2026-10-09 live-board add + confirm)

## Attribution

Every parts order must be attributed to **one or more customers**.

- If the customer is unknown, **ask Ryan**.
- Do **not** guess the customer.
- Do **not** label an order as inventory unless Ryan **explicitly** says it is inventory.

## Add to the live board

**Confirmed 2026-10-09 by Ryan White.**

Add the part to the **live parts board** whenever:

- a **parts check** runs, or
- **Ryan says who a part is for**

Do not leave that part only in chat, a draft, or a side list.

## Confirm before reporting done

Confirm the part is on the **live board** before anyone reports the parts check / add **done**. If it is not on the live board, it is not done.

## When the part arrives

Delete the board line once that part is received.
