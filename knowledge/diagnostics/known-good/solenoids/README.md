# Solenoids — known-good

File only confirmed-good solenoid captures (coil drive and contact/high-current side, labeled which) after the cart was proven healthy. Each file must record year/make/model, solenoid role (main, reverse, precharge, or other stated), test points, and meter/scope settings, plus the known-good criteria used: **voltage, timing, shape, stability, frequency, load response**, and how coil voltage relates to key/F-N-R, throttle, and controller enable. Do not store a trace here until those criteria were met. No customer PII, job numbers, or invented waveforms.
