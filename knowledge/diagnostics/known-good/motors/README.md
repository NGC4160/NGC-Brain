# Motors — known-good

File only confirmed-good motor captures (series wound, separately excited, AC/PMSM, or other type stated on the capture) after the cart was proven healthy. Each file must record year/make/model, motor type, test points, and meter/scope settings, plus the known-good criteria used: **voltage, timing, shape, stability, frequency, load response**, and how the motor current/voltage relates to throttle, solenoid, and controller outputs. Do not store a trace here until those criteria were met on a working cart. No customer PII, job numbers, or invented waveforms.
