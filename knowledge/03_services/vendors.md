## Approved Vendor Register (standing)

**Confirmed 2026-10-09 by Ryan White.**

Every **price-book item** must come from an approved vendor on the **Approved Vendor Register**.

The **Pricebook** bot owns the price book / vendor register revamp (duplicates, unused items, pricing to tier, labor, cost tracking). Register and price-book **changes happen only with Ryan’s approval of specific lists**. Do not invent register rows or silently add a vendor.

If a part is **not** in the price book: source it from a vendor on the register (**part + tax + shipping**, then the live **HCP material markup tier**), with **full ordering notes**. If no register vendor carries the part, use the best source, **preferring dealer-account vendors**, and **ask Ryan to add the vendor**. Full estimate rule: [shop_services.md](shop_services.md#hcp-estimate-rules-standing).

Do **not** invent where the live register file lives. Open the live register or ask Ryan via Chief.
