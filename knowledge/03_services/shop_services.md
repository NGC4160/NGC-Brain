## HCP estimate rules (standing)

**Confirmed 2026-10-09 by Ryan White.** Shop builds the estimate. **Pricebook** owns the price-book catalog and Approved Vendor Register — [pricebook_reference.md](pricebook_reference.md#price-book-policy-standing) / [vendors.md](vendors.md).

**Estimates stay unsent drafts until Ryan reviews them.** Do **not** send an estimate to the customer before that review.

**Tax:** Estimate tax is **always** set to **automated**. Do not invent a tax rate or turn tax off.

### Parts not in the price book

1. Source from a vendor on the **Approved Vendor Register**.
2. Cost the line as **part + tax + shipping**, then apply the live **HCP material markup tier**.
3. Put **full ordering notes** on the estimate / job (vendor, what was quoted, shipping). Do not invent notes.
4. If **no register vendor** carries the part: use the best source, **preferring dealer-account vendors**, and **ask Ryan to add the vendor**.

### Battery core charge

**$20** per **6V or 8V**. **$30** per **12V**. Charge the core **only if NGC does not get the old battery as a core**. Do not invent other core amounts.

### Lithium estimates

Check the photos for a **DC-DC converter with a key-switch trigger wire**. If none, **add one** and **note why** on the estimate. Office quoting packages: [lithium_sales_guide.md](../02_products/lithium_sales_guide.md).

### Brake inspection & adjustment

The brake inspection & adjustment line must say NGC will **adjust what can be adjusted** and **inspect the system to determine if anything else is needed**. Do not invent a promise to replace brakes from that line.

Credit card surcharge still goes on every estimate — [below](#credit-card-surcharge-shop-wide-office-rule).

## Free inspection included

Every service includes the **7-point safety inspection**:

1. Wires, cables, terminals
2. Battery water levels (lead-acid)
3. Lights and horn
4. Tires — inflation, tread, wear
5. Steering and suspension
6. Drivetrain (axle & motor)
7. Brakes

**Printable form:** [`external_docs/templates/operations/NGC_Golf_Cart_Inspection_Report.pdf`](../../external_docs/templates/operations/NGC_Golf_Cart_Inspection_Report.pdf) — battery Δ / mOhm grid + Pass/Fail/N/A checklist. Used for complimentary campground / event inspections; promo **INSPECT10** = 10% off any service when the sheet is presented at booking.
